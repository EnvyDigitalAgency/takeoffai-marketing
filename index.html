What it actually does

You upload the whole PDF binder. TakeoffAI works out which sheets are electrical, reads the legend on those sheets, and counts what it finds. Every item it counts gets a marker on the drawing at the spot it was found.

Where the drawing is unclear, it writes a note instead of guessing. In the screenshot above that's the notes down the left: a GPO count that's approximate because the symbols are packed together, and a legend sheet that wasn't in the upload.

You go through the markers, fix whatever it got wrong, and confirm each page. Then you export a bill of quantities to Excel or PDF, or push a priced quote into Simpro.
