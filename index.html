Residential, commercial and industrial, we've got you covered.

TakeoffAI is made for the electrical take-off: it reads the legend on your drawings, works out what needs counting, and counts it. You review the markers on the plan and export.

Simpro integration

Count in TakeoffAI.
Quote in Simpro.

TakeoffAI connects to Simpro. Once you've reviewed the take-off it's priced against your own rates and pushed into Simpro as a quote you can send. No CSV export, no re-keying a bill of quantities.

  • Priced before it lands. Counts are costed against your price lists and labour rates, so what arrives in Simpro is a quote, not a parts list.
  • No transcription errors. The number on the drawing is the number on the quote.
  • Included free. The Simpro connection is part of the free platform. You only ever pay for the AI take-off itself.
