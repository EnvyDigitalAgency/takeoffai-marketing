Pricing FAQ

What happens after the 7-day trial?
After 7 days you'll be prompted to pick a plan. If you don't subscribe, your account pauses — your projects and data are still there if you come back. We never auto-charge without your consent.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time from your account settings. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you're only charged the difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.
Is there a contract or minimum term?
No contracts. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current month. Annual plans are covered by the 30-day unconditional guarantee, and the 90-day outcome guarantee on top of that.
What does the annual in-house estimator include?
Annual Pro members get a dedicated in-house electrical estimator who will personally run up to 5 full takeoffs for you each month — reviewed and bid-ready — on top of your unlimited AI takeoffs. Great for overflow weeks, tricky drawing sets, or a second set of eyes before you bid.
Do you offer discounts for larger teams?
Yes. If you're running a larger estimating team, reach out to us at support@takeoff.ai and we'll put together a custom package.
