The honest breakdown

Where each tool actually wins

We're not going to pretend every competitor is rubbish. Here's the real picture.

TakeoffAI

Best for: having the counting done for you

If you want to upload a PDF and have the AI count everything, then check its markers on the drawing, TakeoffAI is the only option on this list. Free account, no card, first take-off in minutes, no database setup. Built for Australian electrical contractors by ex-tradies, and it connects to Simpro.

Groundplan

Best for: a native mobile app on site

Groundplan is a solid Australian product. If you need a native mobile app on site it has a real edge over TakeoffAI right now. Both connect to Simpro, so that's no longer a reason to pick one over the other. The key limitation: there's no AI counting. You still click every symbol; it's just faster than paper.

ConEst

Best for: large US-style estimating departments

ConEst is a powerful, enterprise-grade platform built for large US electrical contractors with a dedicated estimating team and budget to match. For Australian sole traders or SMEs it's overkill: expensive, complex to configure and not built with AU standards in mind.

Bluebeam Revu

Best for: general PDF mark-up, not take-offs

Bluebeam is excellent at PDF collaboration and mark-up. But it's not a take-off tool. There's no BOQ export, no AI counting and no electrical symbol library. Many contractors use it alongside a take-off tool for annotation, not instead of one.
