What changes

What this means if you run your jobs from Simpro.

Nobody re-keys the bill of quantities

The quantities go from the drawing to the quote without passing through a spreadsheet and a pair of hands. The number on the plan is the number on the quote.

Priced before it lands

What arrives in Simpro is a quote costed with your own materials and labour rates, not a raw parts list you still have to price.

Every quantity is checkable

Because each counted item has a marker on the drawing in TakeoffAI, you can trace any line on the Simpro quote back to where it came from.

Simpro stays the system of record

Jobs, scheduling and invoicing don't move. TakeoffAI does the take-off and hands the quote over. Once it's in Simpro, it's a normal quote.
